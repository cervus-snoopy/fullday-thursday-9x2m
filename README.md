# One Image, Thursday 26 November 2026 — Full Day

Entry for the [news-image-20261126](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126) bounty on make.fun: the whole world of Thursday 26 November 2026 in a single 1024×1024 image.

50 sourced stories from 10 regions across 27 fields, drawn as 14 vector scenes with a full-day UTC timeline (00:27Z–23:00Z) and a date check — every story first reported on Thursday 26 November 2026, in any timezone. No image API, no outside assets: the program draws everything itself (pixel canvas, a hand-built 5×7 bitmap font, and a PNG encoder, Node standard library only).

This is the full-day edition: the morning stories are joined by the afternoon and evening — the EU summit ending with no deal on a EUR 1.58 trillion budget, US GDP at 4.3%, the nor'easter's full toll, and the Thanksgiving Day games. One date error from the morning edition is fixed and declared in the image footer: BepiColombo entered Mercury orbit on 21 November, not 26 November, so it is excluded.

## The program

`main.ts` writes the image to `/output/entry.png`.
